**The first release of Stampy · 小章第一版**

A tiny stamp for your copies: click once in Preview to stamp "For XX use only" on a copy. Your original stays untouched.
给复印件，盖个小小的章：在预览里点一下，就给副本盖上“仅供××使用”。原件不动。

### Download · 下载

**[Stampy.dmg](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-stampy/releases/download/v1.0.0/Stampy.dmg)** (2.5 MB) — open it and drag Stampy into Applications. 打开后把小章拖进应用程序。

- macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon & Intel · macOS 14 及以上，Apple 芯片和 Intel 都能用
- Signed and notarized by Apple · 已签名并经过 Apple 公证
- Free, no sign-up, no ads · 免费，不用注册，没有广告

### What's in 1.0 · 1.0 有什么

- Stamp right in Preview — PDFs and photos, every page or just one, password-protected PDFs too
  在预览里直接盖：PDF 和照片，可以只盖一页或每一页都盖，加了密码的 PDF 也能盖
- A whole stack at once — drag files or a folder onto the menu bar icon
  一沓一次盖完：把文件或文件夹拖到菜单栏图标上
- Stamp workshop with 17 templates, in Chinese or English · 刻章工坊，17 个模板，中文英文都有
- A date and ID on every stamp, with a history you can check · 每次盖章都有日期和编号，记录随时查
- Chinese and English interface · 中英文界面
- Nothing is uploaded; it only goes online when you click "Check for Updates…"
  文件不上传；只有点“检查更新…”时才会连一下网站
